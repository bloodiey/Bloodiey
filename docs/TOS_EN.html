Bloodiey's Art ToS for art commissions (Last Updated: 06/2026)

Chapter 1: Do's and Don'ts

1.1: I will not draw third-party characters unless they are original characters that are not tied to any third-party franchise (e.g. Sonic the Hedgehog, Lord X, Mario Bros, MX, etc.)

1.1.1: This does not apply to company logos, software programs, or non-animated products (e.g. video game consoles, liquid soap, Firefox).

1.1.2: Geometry Dash cubes, Minecraft skins, Roblox avatars, and Gacha OCs are allowed as long as they are not characters like those mentioned above, e.g. Steve, Alex.

1.1.3: Fan art of YouTubers is allowed as long as they have not been involved in deplorable behavior in the past (e.g. GuitarHeroStyles, MAAU).

1.2: Fetishized content is not allowed at this time, including foot tickling, extreme obesity, vore, pregnancies, flatulence ("farts"), and other fetishes I will not list.

1.2.1: "Paws" and "maws" furries do not fall under this category unless they involve tickling or fetishistic acts.

1.2.1.1: Kissing is allowed as long as it is not in inappropriate places.

1.3: NSFW art is allowed; however, it will not be published publicly.

1.4: Underage characters may not have their torso exposed.

1.5: "Feral" characters may not have suggestive traits, and their genitals must not be shown.

1.6: Political banners are allowed as long as they are to my liking (I will not promote right-wing propaganda or support for Israel, for example).

1.6.1: No propaganda for any candidate or political party will be created, even if I personally agree with the ideology.

1.6.1.1: Humorous content about a politician is not covered by this rule as long as it is not related to elections or sensitive topics (e.g. "welfare mode", "welfare furries", "memes about communism or the USSR").

1.6.2: No commission related to protests will be made; if I want to protest, I will do it myself.

1.7: Use common sense.

Chapter 2: Copyright

2.1: The content may be used freely by the client.

2.2: If the client becomes exposed, the content will become public domain.

2.2.1: If I am the one exposed, the content will be yours.

2.3: If the content includes a source file (e.g. a 3D model, code, or template), it may not be shared on forums, the internet, or P2P networks.

2.4: If this clause is violated, I reserve the right to file a copyright claim (except for point 2.2).

Chapter 3: Prices

3.1: The final price may be higher or lower depending on the circumstances or the effort required.

3.2: Refunds cannot be made once the content is completed, specifically after the last revision.

3.3: The commission must be paid once the agreement is reached.

3.4: Commissions related to politics will cost 50% more (e.g. "political jokes").

3.4.1: QUEER drawings are not covered by this rule.
